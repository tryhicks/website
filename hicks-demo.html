Elevator Pitch

Hicks is a local-first AI knowledge base for solo entrepreneurs. It's your second brain — it reads everything you write, connects the dots, and answers questions about your work without you having to dig through notes. No cloud dependency. No subscription for your own data. Just a coxswain for your thinking.

Core Problem

Solo founders and small teams generate hundreds of notes, decisions, and ideas every week. They lose track of what they decided, why they decided it, and what's connected to what. Existing tools (Notion, Obsidian, Roam) are filing cabinets — they store but don't think. Hicks is different: it's a personal knowledge base that actively connects your thinking.

Key Insight

The best second brain is the one you don't have to maintain. Hicks works from the content & SEO — it processes your notes locally, extracts topics, entities, and relationships, and builds a living graph of your thinking. It doesn't just store — it connects.

Target Users

Solo entrepreneurs who wear every hat. They're evaluating user personas like "Alex the solo founder" who has 15 meetings a week, writes daily notes, and needs an AI knowledge base that surfaces what matters without being asked.

Business Model

Freemium: free tier (5 AI queries/day, basic knowledge graph), Pro ($19/mo — unlimited queries, full knowledge graph, custom skills), Team ($49/mo — shared knowledge base, team analytics). See pricing model for full breakdown.

Competitive Landscape

Obsidian, Notion, Roam, Logseq — all great filing cabinets. None of them actively read your notes and answer questions. That's the gap. See competitive analysis for the full matrix.

"Your notes don't need a home. They need a coxswain."

Architecture

We use a local embedding model for semantic understanding, a small LLM for entity extraction and relationship mapping, and a graph database for the knowledge base. The marketing strategy doc covers why we chose local-first over cloud. The content & SEO doc covers the processing chain.
